1. How the consensus was built

Which scenes. Each development event gets up to its 3 nearest valid neighbouring passes within ±10 days. These are the Stage 11 production-gated passes with ≥ 5 valid water pixels, excluding the matched pass.

  • The run. 2,487 neighbour scenes for 1,215 events, run through the unchanged K4 ACOLITE path: 2,072 L1C items, 15 failures (6 had no L1C).
  • The predictor. Each scene is predicted by the fold C-gbm trained without its lake.

How each scene is weighted. Each scene carries its prediction, tier, platform and Δt. Its variance is σ²_tier + κ·|Δt|, fitted on training lakes:

  • σ ≈ 0.19 (tier 1) and 0.17–0.20 (tier 2);
  • κ ≈ 0.0015–0.0020 per day.

That growth is small: a scene 10 days away is only about 20% noisier than the matched one.

Calibration. Posteriors are re-calibrated (nested) by the number of contributing scenes, and the frozen Stage 16 gate (0.70 / 0.70) issues the state.

What agreement buys.

  • The rule: at least 2 compatible full-authority scenes whose states contain the answer, no disagreeing scene, and a posterior ≥ 0.90.
  • What it gives: at ±10 days, Strong rises from Stage 16's 11% to 21–24% of usable answers, at 95% right. With only ±3 days (the selected variant) it stays at 11%.
  • The target is out of reach. It is not close to 50%: half of events have at most one valid neighbour (23% have none), and agreement often still leaves the posterior below 0.90.
  • Why not loosen it. Lowering the bar to hit 50% would trade accuracy for coverage, which the pre-registration forbids.

6. Sentinel-2C

S2C AGREEMENT CAN UPGRADE AUTHORITY: NO.

  • The test. S-agree against S-off, at the selected variant.
  • The result. S2C raised 0 answers' evidence level, against the 30 required. Development contains almost no S2C: 49 of 2,072 neighbour scenes, and 38 anchors.
  • What it would take. Adding agreeing S2C scenes changes the consensus very little (M-bayes S2C-agree against S2C-off: 0.745 against 0.745). The question can only be answered on 2025–2026 data, where S2C is a third of scenes.
  • No S2C scene regains authority alone, and no S2C bias correction was fitted.
