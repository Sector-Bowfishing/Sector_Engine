The answer

The new reflectance improves the clarity estimate wherever it can read the water. It reads only three stations in four, and it does not fix the red band.

  • The configuration. ACOLITE with the FFSS sky-dome interface correction (K4), surface pressure from each lake's elevation, and S2C harmonised at the water-reflectance level.
  • The gain. C3 refitted on this reflectance is the best model, and no simpler one is equivalent: held-out error ×1.47, against ×1.66 for today's production on the same 1,206 rows at 792 lakes.
    • The reflectance itself buys most of it. The same C3 on Sen2Cor scores ×1.58 on those rows (difference −0.031, CI −0.045 to −0.016).
    • Clear water is where it shows: ×1.56 against ×1.97, and a median error of −3.3 ft against −5.2 ft for production. Routing adds nothing (0.8%).
  • What it does not fix:
    • Coverage. ACOLITE gives a valid disk at only 76% of stations. Sen2Cor has one at all of them.
    • The red band. No interface option cut the red bias enough (K4 +0.22 against +0.25). The two that cut it to +0.15 lost too many retrievals.
    • Turbidity. It is not better overall: better in clear water, worse in murky water.
    • Sentinel-2C. It is only partly harmonised, and the engine's class still flips on 11% of identical S2A/S2C pixels.
  • Capability, historical and strict:
    • exact feet 0/10;
    • clarity class 1/10 (clear and sediment PARTIAL).
    • Stained and limited water are the weakest classes on every input tested (recall 0.2–0.3).
  • The blind certification set was built without reading a single Secchi value: 289 observations, 272 lakes, 39 states. It is not ready: short of 400 observations, of stained, sediment and limited water, and of the cool season.

2. Deviations and addenda

  • A (harmonisation, 15:04Z). A technical check on one pair showed that TOA harmonisation does not carry through ACOLITE's dark-spectrum aerosol step. The addendum added a water-reflectance-level method (V-ρw). It also made the ACOLITE-path verdict use the method that would be adopted operationally, not the V-full emulation.
  • B and B2 (certification matching, 15:31Z and 16:32Z).
    • Earth Search's production collection has no scenes at all for 2017 or 2022, which are the two NLA survey years.
    • Matching for those years uses Earth Search's older sentinel-2-l2a collection.
    • B's first reading of that collection's offset flag was wrong: it double-subtracted 0.1 from 2022. B2 corrected it before any match was counted.
  • C (surface pressure, 15:59Z).
    • At a 2,318 m lake, every pixel was flagged negative, because ACOLITE had used sea-level pressure.
    • Every corpus crop now sets its elevation from USGS 3DEP. ACOLITE converts it to pressure.
    • The 59 rows already processed were discarded.
    • AERONET sites sit at 0–174 m, so Part 1 is unaffected. At Lake Erie, B04 changes by +2% and the pooled error by +0.013.
  • D (viewing geometry, 17:23Z). Older L1C baselines keep detector footprints as GML, which the GDAL stand-in cannot read. Those crops fall back to ACOLITE's angle-grid geometry.
  • Smaller notes.
    • The NLA 2022 site file writes the sampled flag as YES, not Y.
    • SYKE was queried in bulk rather than lake by lake, under the same rules.
    • A candidate that "fails to run" means an ACOLITE error or an all-NaN output. Losing valid station pixels is judged by criterion 3, not as a failure to run.

Sen2Cor production path: the bands are fixed, but P2's decisions still flip.

  • The bands. Harmonisation brings every band's held-out median within 2%: B05 1.010, B8A 1.001, B11 0.993, B12 0.981.
  • The decisions. P2 still flips on 8.1% of identical-water pixels, down from 24.1%. The flips concentrate in turbid pairs (Pontchartrain, Mobile Bay 11–18%).
  • Most likely why: P2's residual test reacts to the pixel-level scatter between two sensors, which harmonising band medians cannot remove.

ACOLITE path: the adopted method (V-ρw) brings flips under the bar.

  • Flips. P2's decisions on ACOLITE reflectance flip on 3.9% of held-out pixels, down from 22.3% raw.
  • Residuals. B02–B05 and B8A are within 1.5%.
  • The TOA method (V-op) is worse at 8.8% (Tampa Bay 62%). It aligns the band medians, but not the dark pixels that ACOLITE's aerosol step reads.
  • The residual that misses: B11, at −0.0006 against a ±0.0005 bar. ACOLITE sets water B11 to about zero, so the band carries little for water.
  • Coverage. Two pairs, Lake Volta and Lake Nasser, have no water pixel valid in every run, so 10 of 12 pairs are scored.

S2C HARMONIZED: PARTIAL, by the frozen rule. Only one path is under 5%, and one ACOLITE residual (B11) misses its bar.

The caveat that matters.

  • Only the commissioning tandem is public. V-ρw raises S2C's visible water reflectance by about 0.002, because commissioning S2C read darker through ACOLITE. Operational S2C may not need that.
  • Where it gets tested. The corpus platform test (section 6) checks it on 2025 S2C.
