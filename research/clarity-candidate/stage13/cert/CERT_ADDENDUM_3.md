# Certification protocol, addendum 3 (Stage 13): the extraction budget

Recorded after the value-blind prescreen and before any new extraction. No Secchi value has been fetched.

**Why.** The prescreen passed 4,898 new SYKE events (687 cool-season). At the Stage 12 rate for Finnish scenes, which
fall under many overlapping passes within ±1 day, extracting all of them takes about 15 hours.

**This does not change readiness.** Its per-class rule is carried by the US core (NLA), and Finnish events cannot fill the
US-core stained, sediment and limited minima.

**Change.** Extract every cool-season event that passed (687), plus 1,500 warm-season events drawn at random with seed
20261003. Everything else, including the matching rules and the per-lake cap filled cool-season first, is unchanged.
Events not drawn stay in `in_s13_*.csv` for later.
