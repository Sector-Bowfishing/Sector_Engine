# Brain certification — duplication inventory (D-7b, before extraction)

**Scope:** the structural-certification machinery of the three certified Brains at `origin/main` `0335d228`: Wind (Repository Phase 2), Fish (Fish Structural Certification V1), Water Clarity (Water Clarity Structural Certification V1). **The copies were not identical.** Wind never had a single certification file: its structural proofs are spread over `verification/integration/test_wind_package.py` (identity, contract, package, staging) and `brains/wind/tests/test_publish_artifact.py` (Gate-P artifact/Publisher). Fish and Clarity each have one `test_<brain>_certification.py` (≈ 518 / 677 lines) plus a `*_support.py` rig. Three `describe_contract.py` files (54 / 60 / 65 lines) share one skeleton.

Classification: **IDENTICAL** (same assertion, no Brain value) · **PARAMETER_ONLY** (same assertion, Brain supplies values) · **DOMAIN_SPECIFIC** (about what the Brain knows or its own mechanics) · **PLATFORM_SPECIFIC** (about the platform, kept in the platform suites) · **LEGACY_COMPATIBILITY** (a legacy table/path still being retired) · **SHOULD_NOT_BE_SHARED** (sharing would hide a real difference).
