## One-paragraph truth
There are **two different winds** at Sector. **Production** (what customers see) is Open-Meteo Best Match (HRRR → GFS) through the Engine `/conditions` endpoint, one lake-wide reading, unscored. The **Wind Brain** is a *shadow* candidate (NOAA NBM v5.0 + RTMA-RU + METAR + frozen fetch tables) publishing to the Brain RTDB for 4 lakes. It is **not promoted** and is not what the app shows. Its certification score is **3.1 / 10**; its secondary Bowfishing Wind Intelligence Score (BWIS v1) is **5.4 / 10**.

## Findings that shape this campaign
1. **The Brain is already further along on bank exposure than on wind truth.** Frozen fetch tables, CEM chop, bank rank (PROTECTED → EXTREME), best/worst banks and nearby alternatives exist for 4 lakes (+70 tables). They are not customer-facing and not published.
2. **What customers see is not the Brain's wind.** Until production is moved (Michael's decision, open since Stage 3D.1), any Brain improvement is invisible.
3. **Two honest holes:** no bank-pair truth (B3/D2 = 17 BWIS pts) and no over-water anemometer (cap 7).
4. **Coverage is the cheapest big win.** 547 of 621 lakes have no fetch geometry although 599 have a polygon. See `WIND_FLEET_SCALING.md`.
5. **Rubric concern:** see `WIND_SCORE_LADDER.md` (event and all-hours-direction conditions reward things bowfishing mostly does not use).
