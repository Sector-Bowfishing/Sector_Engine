# Depth and Habitat (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

- Habitat classes with the strongest support: shallow vegetated flats and flooded vegetation (carp, gars, bowfin, bigmouth, snakehead, grass carp), connected backwaters/oxbows (bigmouth, shortnose gar, spotted gar), tributary mouths (carp, grass carp, smallmouth buffalo, silver carp), tailwaters (longnose gar, buffalo, silver carp schools).
- Spawning depths are well documented (about 0.3-1.6 m across the plant-spawning species).
- Almost every non-spawning "depth" is bottom depth at the fish fix, not swimming depth; shore distance in the shootable zone is reported by almost no study.
- No non-spawning depth/shore-distance data exist for a southeastern reservoir for any species; Intelligence should use habitat-class rules and leave depth bands to the Depth Brain.
