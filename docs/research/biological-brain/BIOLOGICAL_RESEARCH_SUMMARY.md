## Key discoveries (direction only; detail is private)
1. **Seasonality is archetypal, not generic.** Warm-rise (≥~15 C + rising stage) is the most reusable movement trigger across gars, grass carp, buffalo and silver carp; a rise without warmth does not move gars.
2. **Silver carp follow flow**, not temperature, in four independent datasets; spawn cues are not met in pooled TVA water. Boats trigger jumping, mostly after the hull passes and away from it.
3. **Gars spawn in 0.3-1.6 m flooded vegetation**; an agency review flags spawn-area gar as highly vulnerable to bowfishing. Longnose gar spawn below dams in the one Southeast source.
4. **Bigmouth buffalo need level plus temperature**; fast recession can destroy a year class. Smallmouth buffalo pass spillways only when inundated.
5. **Grass carp feed and stay shallow in winter** (contradicts older pond-derived feeding-onset figures) and are bound to submerged vegetation; "leave when grass is gone" is untested.
6. **Night evidence is thin and contradictory:** direct support for spotted gar movement, grass carp mobility, smallmouth buffalo (one dataset); null for alligator and longnose gar telemetry; snakehead diurnal; none for bigmouth/black buffalo.
7. **Oxygen response** is undocumented in the field for every species.
8. Production species table differs from literature in a few strings and temperatures (flagged, not changed).
