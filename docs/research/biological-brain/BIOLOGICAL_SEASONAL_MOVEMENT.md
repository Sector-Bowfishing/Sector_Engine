# Seasonal Movement (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

Main result: **there is no single seasonal model.** Five archetypes emerged: (A) thermal-cue flats spawners, (B) flood-pulse floodplain spawners, (C) riverine-run spawners following discharge, (D) tailwater/lock-and-dam stagers, (E) shallow-vegetation residents. Silver carp, alligator gar and black buffalo violate the "winter basin → spring shallows" template.

- Three distinct early-spring triggers: a temperature gate, temperature-conditioned discharge (gars: a rise without warmth does nothing), and discharge alone (silver carp).
- Postspawn and fall are the least-studied phases for almost every species.
- Summer rules must be conditional (flow for silver carp; species-specific elsewhere); summer-night position in southeastern pools is unsourced for every species.
- Candidate rule families for Intelligence are listed in the private document; all are "species-supported; local transfer untested".
