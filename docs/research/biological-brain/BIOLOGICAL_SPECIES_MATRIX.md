# Species Knowledge Matrix (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

Matrix covers 25 species rows (29 registry rows; 4 are group or duplicate rows) across identity, season, movement, temperature, depth, habitat, vegetation, clarity, flow, time of day, spawn and bowfishing relevance, with direct/inferred flags and a Southeast-transfer tag.

- **Tier 1 (matrix complete):** common, grass, silver, bighead carp; smallmouth, bigmouth, black buffalo; river carpsucker/quillback/highfin; alligator, longnose, spotted, shortnose gar; bowfin.
- **Tier 2 (partial):** northern snakehead, black carp, goldfish, freshwater drum.
- **Tier 3 (profile-level):** gizzard shad, channel catfish, paddlefish, blue sucker, four redhorses, American shad, striped mullet, tilapia.
- Every cell is labelled direct or inferred, and carries a transfer tag (TRANSFERS / PARTIAL / UNTESTED). The tag distribution: nearly all night and summer cells for principal species are UNTESTED.
- Score distribution of the proposals is in `BIOLOGICAL_SCORE_HISTORY.md`.
