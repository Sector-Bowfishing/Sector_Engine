# Temperature Preferences and Cues (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

- Spawn / movement-onset temperatures were found, with ranges preserved, for about 18 species; they are the best-supported temperature domain.
- Preference and activity temperatures are mostly lab or compilation level.
- Cold-water activity evidence exists for silver carp and grass carp (feeding in winter), contradicting older pond-derived feeding-onset figures.
- **Field oxygen response is essentially undocumented** for every species; the temperatureOxygen dimension cannot exceed mid-scores on current evidence.
- Southern populations spawn 1-2 months earlier than northern ones; the offset is not quantified, so northern thresholds must not be applied to TVA pools unadjusted.
- Production spawn-temperature table vs literature: several differences flagged in the spawn digest (longnose gar, catfish, bowfin southern start).
