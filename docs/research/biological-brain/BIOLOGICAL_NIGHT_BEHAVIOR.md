# Night Behaviour (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

- Night is the app's main use and the least-supported domain: no species has night depth/shore-position telemetry from a southeastern reservoir.
- Direct night support exists for spotted gar (movement), grass carp (mobile at night), silver carp (boat-triggered jumping, trawl CPUE), smallmouth buffalo (Ohio River, one dataset).
- Null or contrary evidence: alligator gar, longnose gar (telemetry), snakehead (diurnal).
- No evidence: bigmouth and black buffalo, shortnose gar, bowfin.
- All night statements must carry "species-supported; local transfer untested".
