- Spawn is the best-evidenced domain and the one most tied to shallow, shootable water.
- Headline findings: gar spawn in very shallow flooded vegetation and are agency-flagged as highly vulnerable to bowfishing in spring and early summer; silver-carp spawn cues are not temperature-only and are not met in pooled TVA water; bigmouth buffalo need level as well as temperature (spawned in a minority of years in one lake); longnose gar use tailwater rock riffles in the one source that speaks to the Southeast.
- Direct spawning observations are absent for smallmouth and black buffalo (gonad-inferred or single stream).
- **Production comparison (informational only; production unchanged):** the species table is consistent for most species; flags for review are longnose-gar temperature (production above literature), smallmouth-buffalo vegetation wording (unsupported), silver/bighead/grass-carp habitat wording (spawn is in flowing tributaries), bowfin southern start (earlier than the table window), channel-catfish temperature (above the range found).
