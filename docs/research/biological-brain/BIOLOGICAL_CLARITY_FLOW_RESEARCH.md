# Clarity, Turbidity and Flow (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

- Clarity is the weakest dimension; many species are turbidity tolerant (carp, silver carp, bigmouth, shortnose gar); clear-water preference is compilation-level (spotted gar, quillback, highfin).
- **Flow is the strongest cross-species signal**: silver carp (four datasets), grass carp, alligator gar, quillback and others move or spawn on rising stage or discharge; warm rises matter for gars; fast recession can destroy a buffalo year class; dam operations gate access.
- In pooled rivers stage and discharge decouple, so Midwest hydrograph numbers must be used as relative-rise logic, not absolute thresholds.
