# Vegetation Associations (public-safe digest)

> Full species-level detail is in the private research store (`Sector_Research_Data/biological-brain/deliverables/`), outside public git per the Biological Brain contract. This digest records structure, direction and counts only: no per-species rules, thresholds or evidence text.

- Strong associations: grass carp with submerged vegetation (hydrilla), spotted/shortnose gar, bowfin, snakehead, bigmouth buffalo and alligator gar for spawning.
- No vegetation evidence was found for silver, bighead or black carp, or smallmouth/black buffalo; rules must not claim any.
- Edge-versus-interior of beds is unknown biologically for every species (tracking resolution).
- Loss of vegetation depresses grass carp condition and survival; emigration on vegetation loss is untested.
- Vegetation Brain needs: type (submerged/emergent/flooded terrestrial), density, seasonal state.
