# Biological score rubric: where it may not measure product capability (PROPOSAL ONLY; nothing changed)

Per the Product Intelligence Reset: when a dimension demands far more than the capability needs, document the mismatch and propose; do not change the rubric (`biological-state-score-v1`) without approval. Today: published **4.6**, one active gate (no VALIDATED species, cap 6.0). Numbers below use the live published facts and the real rule set (48 usable rules).

| Dimension (weight) | What the rubric measures | What the product needs | Mismatch? | Proposal (Michael decides) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **knowledgeDepth (20)**, now 1.0 | median species-v2 score over **all 27 assessed rows** | enough knowledge about the species Sector actually reports (the 12 Lake-Brain species) to say where/when/under what conditions | **Yes.** 15 of 27 rows are species Sector never rates; they hold the median at 1.0 while the 12 reported species have median 4.0 | Median over the Lake-Brain-scoped species, versioned as `v2`, history restated, `assessedCoverage` on the same basis, and publish the minimum alongside (full analysis: `BIOLOGICAL_KNOWLEDGE_DEPTH_BASIS.md`). **Deferred by Michael; not applied** |
| **evidenceQuality (10)**, now 2.0 | REVIEWED share × 7, VALIDATED share × 10 | trust that each rule honestly summarises its sources | **Partly.** The scale tops out at 7.0 for a fully REVIEWED registry, below the 7.5 production floor, so it can only pass via VALIDATED species even if every rule were reviewed | Keep the dimension; state plainly that the production floor requires VALIDATED species. Optionally add an intermediate step (e.g. REVIEWED plus prospective partial validation). No change recommended before field data |
| **contradictionsGaps (10)**, now 6.6 | mean over species of min(open contradiction entries, 5)/5 | knowing where evidence conflicts and being honest about it | **Yes.** Many entries are conditional differences (system, season, metric), not defects, and a species at the cap gains nothing from partial cleanup. One resolved entry is worth ≈ +0.007 on the score | Rename/reweight as a hygiene measure, or count only *unlabelled* conflicts. Low priority (`CONTRADICTION_REVIEW.md`) |
| **regionalTransfer (5)**, UNKNOWN | hard-coded "not published" | honesty about how far the fish knowledge transfers to Southeast reservoirs, the largest single uncertainty in the research | **Yes: it has no scoring home.** The Brain now publishes the fact (below) but the scorer does not read it | Let the scorer read `transferSummary`. Actual distribution: **0 TRANSFERS, 28 PARTIAL, 20 UNTESTED of 48**. Options: (a) count only TRANSFERS → 0.0 (honest, scored); (b) TRANSFERS + 0.5×PARTIAL → 2.9; (c) keep UNKNOWN. Recommend (a) so the number is not generous before any Southeast validation. Needs a scorer/platform change and approval |
| **biologicalStateImpl (15)**, now 4.0 | IN_PROGRESS 4, LIVE 8, VALIDATED 10 | an evaluated state that exists and is usable internally before it is customer-facing | **Partly.** There is no level between "internal pilot exists" (4) and "LIVE/consumed" (8), although the reset directive wants internal shadow progress to be visible | Add `SHADOW` (Ops-only per-lake state published, not customer-facing, not consumed) ≈ 6. Today's pilot qualifies only if per-lake publication to the Ops-only RTDB path is approved. **Do not relabel IN_PROGRESS as LIVE** |
| **biologicalStateValidation (10)**, now 0 | PARTIAL 5, VALIDATED 10 | evidence that predictions beat chance in the field | **No.** This one measures the right thing. It needs trips and seasons, not rubric changes | none |

## New Biological-side fact (built; publishes with the next approved bundle)
Registry `transferSummary {usableRules, byTransferability{TRANSFERS, PARTIAL, UNTESTED}, note}` and a `facts[]` row "Rules with Southeast-transferable evidence: N of M". The current scorer ignores it, so publishing it cannot move the score (tested).

## Not proposed
No threshold is lowered, no definition changed, no evidence reclassified, no proposed evidence promoted. If the rubric is ever changed, the new version must be decided before the next score is read and the history restated.
