# Wind → Intelligence contract — final proposal (Wind side)

Supersedes `WIND_INTELLIGENCE_CONTRACT.md` (kept as the earlier draft). **Proposal only: the active Intelligence branch is not edited.** Intelligence must only **restate/combine** these fields; it must not compute exposure, fetch, categories, flags, trend or confidence itself. Wind owns direction, strength, forecast, bank exposure and fetch-derived exposure; Depth owns depth, Clarity owns clarity/resuspension interpretation, Vegetation owns vegetation, Biological owns fish behavior. Wind does not decide whether water becomes muddy or a bank is "good".

Fixtures to build consumer tests on: `docs/brains/wind/fixtures/*.json` (generated by the code; a test fails if they drift).

## 2. Customer-simple answers Wind already provides
`tonight.simple(block, bank)` → `{wind, tonight, risk, trend, bank, fetch, confidence, caveats}`. Intelligence may use these words verbatim; `None` always means "no claim".

## 3. Rules for Intelligence (what it may and may not do)
1. **Restate or combine; never recompute.** No re-deriving category, flag, label or confidence from raw winds.
2. **Unknown stays unknown.** `null`/UNKNOWN/UNSURE ⇒ no sentence. UNKNOWN banks are never PROTECTED.
3. **Do not harden claims**: ESTIMATED is "estimate"; MODELED is never "measured"; `ifItBlows` is not a maximum; no flag is not "low risk"; HIGH confidence does not mean "won't blow" (it ranks direction reliability, not strong-wind risk: HIGH 7.2% vs MEDIUM 8.2% breezy hours).
4. **Combining with other Brains happens in Intelligence only.** Example: Wind says DIRECT + HIGH FETCH; Depth says shallow flat; Clarity says high resuspension sensitivity ⇒ Intelligence may penalise that area. Wind states only the wind/exposure fact.
5. Treat `stale=true`, `confidence=LOW`, or missing `tonight` as "Wind unavailable" and degrade gracefully (`degradedInputs[]`).
6. The legacy `trend` field (±2 mph at +3 h; includes `rising`) is unvalidated; use `tonight.trend`.

## 4. Versioning and change control
`tonight.schema = sector-wind-tonight-v1`; state schema id unchanged (additive). Renames/semantic changes require `-v2` plus new fixtures. The category edges, flag threshold (10 mph), window (19:00–01:00 local) and confidence rule are frozen by the H2 pre-registration; changing them requires a new pre-registration and a new holdout story, so Intelligence should treat them as constants of the contract version.
