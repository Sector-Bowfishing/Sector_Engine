## What Ops can see today vs what Michael asked to see
Ops shows: certification **3.1**, BWIS 5.4, CALIBRATION/PROFILED/DISCOVERED counts, the models strip, health/stale, validation PARTIAL with seven "gates not yet passed" strings and the H2 text. It **cannot** show: 5/30 live days, the gate date, 528 label-eligible lakes, AUDITED/ESTIMATED/BLOCKED counts, field-validation progress, the tonight contract, dual-logging state, a categorised blocker list, or that the computed score is already 3.3.
