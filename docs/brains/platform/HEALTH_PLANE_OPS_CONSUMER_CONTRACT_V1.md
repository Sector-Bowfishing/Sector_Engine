# Health Plane — Sector Ops / Canvas consumer contract V1 (`control/healthV1`)

**Owner of the data:** Sector Engine / Brain Platform (writer `brain-platform`). **Consumer:** Sector Ops / Canvas, read-only under the existing `/control` rules. **Status:** written 2026-10-17 for the future Ops/Canvas session; this is the input to that handoff. Engine owns truth, Ops owns presentation: **Ops computes none of this and keeps no table of Brains, dependencies or signals.** Model contract: `ORCHESTRATOR_HEALTH_V1.md` (§1–§5, §10). Lineage: `ACTUAL_CONSUMPTION_LINEAGE_V1.md`.

## 2. Brain nodes — `brains/{brainId}`
Five concepts, **never collapse them into one light**:
- `process` — did the job run (`processHealth` ∈ HEALTHY WARNING DEGRADED FAILED UNKNOWN NOT_CONFIGURED; `processHealthReasons[]`; `lastExecution` / `lastSuccessful` / `lastFailed` {executionId, executionStatus ∈ RUNNING SUCCEEDED FAILED SKIPPED DEGRADED UNKNOWN, startedAt, endedAt, trigger, codeVersion?}; `lastSuccessfulAt`; `consecutiveFailures`; `codeVersions[]`; `brainReportedHealth`; `cadence {status ∈ ON_CADENCE OVERDUE NOT_EXPECTED UNKNOWN, expectedIntervalHours, overdueAfterHours, lastSuccessAgeHours, reason}`). `NOT_CONFIGURED` = registered Brain without a runtime (Depth, Vegetation today).
- `dependencies` — `dependencyHealth` ∈ SATISFIED DEGRADED UNSATISFIED UNKNOWN; `freshnessHealth` ∈ FRESH STALE EXPIRED UNAVAILABLE UNKNOWN; `declared` (count); `counts {byStatus, byFreshness, byType}`; `rows[]` (one per declared dependency: `dependencyId`, `dependencyType`, `requirement`, `requirementDeclared?`, `availability`, `freshnessStatus`, `freshnessAuthority`, `dependencyStatus`, `evaluationBasis` ∈ RECORDED_CONSUMPTION | CURRENT_STATE, `evaluatedAt`, `consumedAt?`, `lineageRef {ref, stateVersion?, fingerprint?, artifactId?}`, evidence timestamps, `ageHoursAtConsumption?`, `legacy?`, `reasons[]`); `declaredVsActual {consumptionRecorded, declaredAndConsumed[], declaredNotConsumed[], consumedNotDeclared[]}`; `basis` ∈ RECORDED_CONSUMPTION | CURRENT_STATE.
- `freshness` — `{status, basis, note}`: evidence freshness of the REQUIRED dependencies. **Independent of `process.lastSuccessfulAt`.**
- `domainValidation` — `{status, source, reason?}` pass-through of the Brain's own verdict (model v1 words + UNKNOWN). Scientific trust, shown beside health, **never folded into any light**.
- `publish` — `{status ∈ REJECTED SHADOW SKIPPED_UNCHANGED PUBLISHED NOT_APPLICABLE UNKNOWN, …}` pass-through of the Publisher record (UNKNOWN for every Brain until the Publisher is wired).
- `aggregate` — `{status ∈ HEALTHY WARNING DEGRADED FAILED UNKNOWN, worstComponent?, unknownComponents[]?, notConfigured?, inputs{process, dependencies, freshness, publish}}`. Operational presentation helper only: worst **known** component; UNKNOWN process ⇒ UNKNOWN. **Render `unknownComponents` next to the status** ("HEALTHY · freshness, publish unknown"); a HEALTHY aggregate with unknowns is not a green check.

## 3. Dependency graph — `dependencies`
`brains/{brainId} {source ("brain.json" | "control_manifest.json (legacy, platform-authored)"), declarationsKnown, legacy?, rows[]}`, `edges[] {from: "<brain|signal|provider|registry>:<id>", to: brainId, dependencyType ∈ BRAIN ENVIRONMENTAL_SIGNAL PROVIDER_DATA PLATFORM_REGISTRY, requirement?, legacy?}`, `warnings[]`. This is the **only** source of "A depends on B" for Canvas. Draw an edge per entry; a `legacy: true` edge is platform-authored and retires when that Brain registers a `brain.json`. `summary.unknownDeclarations[]` lists Brains with no declaration anywhere (render as unknown, not as "no dependencies"). Actual consumption of an edge = `brains/{to}.dependencies.rows[from]` (`evaluationBasis`, `lineageRef`, `consumedAt`) + `declaredVsActual`.

## 4. Signals — `signals/{signalId}`
`{signalId, displayName, status ∈ DECLARED AVAILABLE RETIRED, unit?, producer {kind, id}?, stalenessModel?}` — definitions only, **never consumers** (consumers are the Brains whose `rows[]` have `dependencyType: ENVIRONMENTAL_SIGNAL`). Today all 7 are `DECLARED` with no producer: a Brain consuming one shows `availability: MISSING`, `freshnessStatus: UNAVAILABLE`. Nothing observes a signal at runtime; do not render a value.

## 5. Declared vs actual
`basis: RECORDED_CONSUMPTION` ⇒ the rows were judged **at the time of consumption** against the state the execution really read (historical truth; a later upstream refresh does not change them). `basis: CURRENT_STATE` ⇒ no lineage was recorded for the last execution; rows describe what the Engine can read now. Both bases coexist during migration; show which one applies. Today (2026-10-17) every row is CURRENT_STATE: lineage recording is deployed by this phase and appears after the next in-process Brain run; Wind's running job records none until the Publisher cutover.

## 6. Display-only fields (render verbatim, never recompute)
Every status word; every `reasons[]`/`reason`; `lastSuccessAgeHours`, `ageHoursAtConsumption`, `overdueAfterHours`, `expectedIntervalHours`; `counts`; `aggregateCounts`; `contentFingerprint`; `codeVersion(s)`; `source`.

## 7. Fields Ops must never derive itself
- Freshness from `lastSuccessfulRun`, `lastRun`, `lastSuccessfulAt` or any job timestamp (process history ≠ evidence freshness).
- Any dependency relationship not present in `dependencies.edges` (no table in Ops/Canvas source; invariant 17).
- Signal availability, a signal value, or a signal's consumers.
- An aggregate across Brains, a "fleet health", or a boolean from `aggregate.status` that hides `unknownComponents`.
- Domain validation or publish status for a Brain that publishes none (UNKNOWN is the value; render a dash).
- Anything from the legacy `control/orchestratorV1.brains.*.freshness` (age of `lastSuccessfulRun`) as evidence freshness.

## 8. Compatibility
`/jobs`, `control/orchestratorV1`, `control/orchestrator`, `health/brains`, `scores`, `brains/*` are unchanged and remain readable; `control/healthV1` is additive. Today's Ops reads keep working. Migration of Ops to the health plane is a separate, future session.
