## 2. Extra asks to paste into specific emails (text only)

Reference documents written by other agents: `DEPTH_TVA_DATUM_CONFIRMATION.md` and `DEPTH_DUKE_DATUM_EVIDENCE.md`. Do not wait for them; paste or adapt after reading them.

**Add to the TVA request (row 1), before the closing paragraph:**

> One further question, separate from the survey data: the pool and operating-guide elevations in TVA's published reservoir operating guides, and the daily reservoir elevations TVA reports for the same reservoirs, are we correct to treat both as the same vertical datum? Could you confirm which datum that is (NGVD29 or NAVD88, or a TVA-specific reference), whether it is the same for every mainstem and tributary reservoir, and whether the datum was changed on any reservoir? We use this only to compare current water levels with full-pool and winter-pool references.

**Add to the Duke request (row 5):**

> We understand Duke publishes lake levels on a local scale where full pond is 100 ft (for example, a reading of 98.5 meaning 1.5 ft below full pond). Could you confirm that "level on the local scale minus 100" equals feet below full pond on each of Lakes Norman, Hickory, James, Wylie, Wateree, Keowee, Jocassee and Robinson, and provide the full-pond elevation (with its vertical datum) for each? That lets us express levels relative to full pond without guessing.

(The 100-scale wording is the fleet doc's open item "verify `shift = scale - 100` with Duke"; it is posed as a question, not a statement of fact.)

Optionally add to every USACE request: "Please also confirm the vertical datum of the pool elevations published for each lake, and the terms for the public CWMS Data API output (see Unlock Queue 4.2)."
