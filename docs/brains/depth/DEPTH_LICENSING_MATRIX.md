# Depth Brain: Contour / Bathymetry / Survey Licensing Matrix

Checked 2026-10-13. Research and documentation only: nothing here was sent to anyone and no production code was touched. This is a data-licence triage by an engineering assistant, **not legal advice**; items marked UNCLEAR or RESTRICTED should go to counsel or to the data owner before any customer-facing use.

Machine-readable twin: `jobs/depth-brain/depth_brain/data/source_licences.csv` (37 rows; `sourceKey` equals the `src` codes in `contour_truth_lakes.csv` / `contour_matches_verified.csv`, e.g. `MN`, `TWDB`, `FL`, `OH`; federal/global sources use the keys listed in section 4).

## 1. Method and rules

* For each source I fetched the licence/terms/metadata that the service itself exposes (ArcGIS item `licenseInfo`, REST `copyrightText`/`description`, FGDC metadata, agency copyright pages, figshare/HydroSHEDS/Copernicus pages). The URL in the table is the page actually fetched. "Paraphrase" means a short paraphrase, not a quotation, unless in quotes.
* Facts not fetched are marked **unverified**. Web-search summaries were used only to locate pages, never as evidence of terms.
* Classes:
  * **PUBLIC_REUSABLE**: an explicit statement of no restriction, CC0, or US-government public-domain policy. A courtesy credit may be requested but is not mandatory.
  * **ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED**: reuse allowed, a credit/citation is expressly required (or the licence is CC BY).
  * **INTERNAL_ONLY**: the terms restrict use to a narrow purpose or to internal use; Sector may analyse but not publish or ship.
  * **UNCLEAR**: no licence, disclaimer-only text, contradictory text, or terms that could not be retrieved. **Defaults to internal-only.**
  * **RESTRICTED**: terms expressly prohibit the use Sector would make (here: commercial use).
* Gate rule for the code: only `classification in {PUBLIC_REUSABLE, ATTRIBUTION_REQUIRED}` and `customerDerivedMetricsAllowed == true` may feed a customer-facing number. `unknown` must be treated as `false`. Nothing UNCLEAR, RESTRICTED or INTERNAL_ONLY reaches a customer product.
* "Derived metrics" = scalar numbers computed from the data (shallow share, mean depth, zone shares, flat hectares). "Raw geometry redistribution" = shipping contours, soundings or DEM cells. "Customer map layer" = rendering the contours/depth bands in the app.
* Common caveat on nearly every row: the owner's disclaimer says the data are not for navigation/safety. Sector must not present any of this as navigational depth.
