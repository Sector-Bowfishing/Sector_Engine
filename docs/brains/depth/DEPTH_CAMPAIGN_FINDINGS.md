## What unlocked lakes (each from public data, each tested)
1. **Duke (8 lakes).** Duke's own lake-levels JSON returns the live level and the full-pond elevation with its datum in one row (`Elevation`: "760.0 ft (AMSL, NGVD 29 datum)"); 7 lakes publish on a 100-based scale. The earlier "HTTP 403" was a wrong route. `shift = Actual - 100` (or absolute - pool); a level in neither frame is refused. (`DEPTH_DUKE_DATUM_EVIDENCE.md`)
2. **Reclamation (16 lakes + Cheney).** RISE also publishes daily storage, which Reclamation computes from the elevation through its capacity table. RISE elevation reproduces RISE storage through the TSC survey tables (9 lakes, offset 0.00 ft) and through the printed capacities of Great Plains allocation diagrams, whose elevations include the pool Depth uses (7 lakes); controls reproduce at <= 0.003 ft. Cheney also pairs with an independent USGS 62614 gauge (-0.025 ft, SD 0.12). Project frames: shift only, never converted. (`DEPTH_RECLAMATION_DATUM_CHAIN.md`)
3. **Vocabulary bug (2 lakes now, 34 directory lakes affected).** The Lake directory spells medium pool confidence `med`; Depth required `medium` and refused them as "low". Normalised at build time (Isabella and Sumner became defensible; the rest were blocked for other reasons). Recorded as a platform-contract input.
4. **LOCAL == NGVD29 proofs (11 lakes)** by a paired USGS series or by the CWMS storage fit, which let their NGVD29 ratings serve LOCAL-tagged series.

## New capability: depth shares from operator ratings (RATING-ZONE-RULES-1)
USACE CWMS `Elev;Area` ratings (87 lakes), `Elev;Stor` derivatives where no area rating exists (23 lakes; validated on 89 lakes that have both: median 0.0016, p90 0.020) and Reclamation survey tables (7 lakes; Heron's table was not monotone and Patterson's disagrees with its contours) give the exact share of the reference-pool surface that is dry now or within 3 / 6 / 10 ft, plus the extra surface flooded above pool. It says how much, never where. Independent contour surveys disagree by a median 0.044 (p90 0.127, max 0.299) on 27 lakes, so every share carries `shareToleranceAbs = 0.13` and lakes with a larger contour disagreement (6) are withheld. The rating vintage is published; 14 have an unknown vintage and 19 are older than 1990. (`DEPTH_STAGE_AREA_RATING_RULES.md`)

## Corrections to earlier claims (disclosed)
* "Duke API returns 403" (wrong route). "RISE link needs Reclamation" (testable from RISE storage). "Hypsometry says nothing about depth" (true for DEM/GLOBathy, not for an operator's published elevation-area rating, which gives the depth-share function exactly).
* Thresholds and rule amendments made after reading results are listed in `DEPTH_STAGE_AREA_RATING_RULES.md` (section 3) and in the scripts' docstrings; none was used to lower a bar to pass a lake.

## Tried and not used
* TVA datum naming: no independent USGS lake gauge within 12 km of any TVA dam (`data/tva_link_test.json`).
* Bounded datum ambiguity (treat an unnamed pool datum as NGVD29-or-NAVD88 and widen sigma): would add 18 lakes at best and silently mis-state any local-datum pool. Rejected.
* Flaming Gorge, Jamestown, Blue Mesa, Fresno (table fit), Box Butte, Buffalo Bill, Glendo, Horsetooth, Granby, Ruedi: inconclusive, left UNKNOWN, reasons in `DEPTH_RECLAMATION_DATUM_REQUEST.md`; the diagram check re-runs by itself when a reservoir refills.

## Upstream finding for Lake Brain (not changed here)
Hartwell's `observationTime` runs about an hour ahead of `generatedAt` (fixed EST offset in EDT). Depth refuses future observations, so Hartwell flaps to `unavailable` for ~45 minutes every hour. See `DEPTH_LAKE_DATUM_DEPENDENCY.md`.
